# Aldermoor Dubai — admissions policy

> **Fictional demo content.** Aldermoor Schools and everything below are invented for this demo. None of it describes a real school, real fees or real requirements.

## Intake dates

- **Main intake:** August, for the academic year starting in late August.
- **Mid-year intake:** January and April, where places are available.
- Applications for the main intake open on 1 September of the previous year.

## Grades offered

KG1 and KG2, then Grade 1 to Grade 12.

## Annual tuition (AED)

| Stage | Range |
|---|---|
| KG1–KG2 | 42,000–48,000 |
| Grades 1–5 | 52,000–60,000 |
| Grades 6–8 | 63,000–70,000 |
| Grades 9–12 | 74,000–85,000 |

A one-off registration fee of 2,500 is charged on acceptance of an offer.

## Required documents

- Completed application form
- Copy of the applicant's passport and residence visa
- Copy of each guardian's passport, residence visa and Emirates ID
- School reports for the last two years (not required for KG1)
- Transfer certificate from the current school (Grade 1 and above)
- Immunisation record
- Two recent passport-size photographs

## Reply standard

Every family gets a reply from the admissions team within **3 working days** of each message or inquiry. Working days are Monday to Friday.
