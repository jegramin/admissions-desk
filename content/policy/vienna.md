# Aldermoor Vienna — admissions policy

> **Fictional demo content.** Aldermoor Schools and everything below are invented for this demo. None of it describes a real school, real fees or real requirements.

## Intake dates

- **Main intake:** September, for the academic year starting in early September.
- **Mid-year intake:** February, where places are available.
- Applications for the main intake open on 1 November of the previous year.

## Grades offered

KG1 and KG2, then Grade 1 to Grade 12.

## Annual tuition (EUR)

| Stage | Range |
|---|---|
| KG1–KG2 | 9,000–10,500 |
| Grades 1–5 | 12,000–14,000 |
| Grades 6–8 | 15,000–17,000 |
| Grades 9–12 | 18,000–21,000 |

A one-off registration fee of 800 is charged on acceptance of an offer.

## Required documents

- Completed application form
- Copy of the applicant's passport or national ID
- Copy of each guardian's passport or national ID
- Proof of residence in Austria (Meldezettel), or a planned move date
- School reports for the last two years (not required for KG1)
- Immunisation record

## Reply standard

Every family gets a reply from the admissions team within **3 working days** of each message or inquiry. Working days are Monday to Friday.
